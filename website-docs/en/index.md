StoryForge is an open-source, local-first tool for narrative creation and experiences. Write fiction, adapt screenplays and comics, prepare motion-comic materials, or build worlds and explore interactive products.

> These guides were checked against main on September 20, 2026. Tagged releases and live deployments may differ. See [current capabilities](/en/features/current-capabilities) and [version compatibility](/en/updates/compatibility).

## Getting started

1. [Choose a product](/en/getting-started/choose-product) for your intended output.
2. [Open the app](/en/getting-started/install) and try a small project with the [quick start](/en/getting-started/quick-start).
3. [Configure a model](/en/getting-started/model-config) when you need AI, confirm candidates, and keep your [first backup](/en/guides/backup-restore).

You do not need the world engine before writing fiction. Start manually or [try built-in works without an API](/en/guides/examples).

## Changes and feedback

The [changelog](/en/updates/changelog) covers independent creation, world versions, redesigned workbenches, and official documentation feedback by main-branch milestone.

Submit a [bug report](/en/feedback/bug), [feature request](/en/feedback/feature), or [documentation correction](/en/feedback/documentation). Include your version and reproduction steps, but remove keys and private manuscripts.

Historical WPS overviews and earlier updates remain in the [archive](/en/archive/) for reference.

# Feature guides

Choose a product by your goal, then read its guide. Each covers entry points, preparation, workflow, outputs, and current boundaries.

## Independent creation

- [Step-by-step longform](/en/features/longform/): ideas and characters through volume/chapter prose and continuity.
- [Short fiction](/en/features/shortform): complete a story around whole-story structure and length.
- [Novel to screenplay](/en/features/screenplay): frozen sources, adaptation, scene writing, and format exports.
- [Novel to comic](/en/features/comic): pages, panels, lettering, and visual delivery.
- [Motion-comic materials](/en/features/motion-drama): episode and shot materials for external video generation.
- [Node authoring (preview)](/en/features/nodes): compose longform actions on a canvas.

## Worlds and interaction

- [World engine](/en/features/world-engine): edit, derive, and seal reusable settings.
- [Interactive products](/en/features/interactive/): TTRPGs, character chat, towns, AVG, and text games in development.
- [Built-in works and examples](/en/guides/examples): read or play before choosing a creative direction.

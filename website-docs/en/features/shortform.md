# Short fiction

For standalone stories of roughly 5,000–25,000 Chinese characters across 3–8 chapters. Entry: Short fiction → Library → New short fiction (短篇 → 作品库 → 新建短篇). The current engineering loop includes creation, review, targeted rewriting, versions, and export.

## Preparation

Prepare a one-sentence premise, target readers, length, core conflict, and intended ending effect. A separate world is not required.

## Complete workflow

1. **Creative intent:** define goals, length, and constraints.
2. **Story design:** establish choices, conflict escalation, and ending; confirm AI candidates.
3. **Chapter cards:** check each chapter's role and continuity; avoid dividing a short story into repetitive episodes.
4. **Prose:** generate, edit, and adopt chapter by chapter; inspect the prose actually saved.
5. **Whole-story review:** examine issues with chapter evidence and select parts for targeted rewriting.
6. **Versions and export:** after completion checks pass, keep a frozen version and export Markdown, TXT, or JSON.

## Ready to deliver?

Check chapter completeness, actual versus target length, pending candidates, and serious issues. After prose changes, an old review may no longer apply and needs rechecking.

Publishing preserves a local frozen version; it does not automatically post the work to a public website.

## Revision and derivation

Targeted rewriting fixes specific chapters while retaining confirmed content elsewhere. Expansion and derivation (扩展与派生) can explicitly derive a world; later world changes do not automatically rewrite the original story.

## Common questions

**Actual length differs from the target?** Check completion results and chapter lengths, adjust chapter tasks, then expand or compress selectively. A target is not a guarantee of precise model counting.

**Generation finished, but publishing is blocked?** Check missing chapters, unadopted candidates, stale reviews, and unresolved serious issues; handle each as prompted.

# D04 · Generate episode-specific character states

Use before an episode outline or script. Extract the current state of the actual cast from the recap, episode outline and full profiles.

```text
You are an experienced short-drama screenwriter. Create a dynamic profile for each character actually appearing in this episode.

A character has a multi-episode arc, but occupies only one stage now. Infer the true starting and ending states from the recap and episode outline; never substitute their future, final characterization.

INPUTS
<Recap>
{{recap_cast_relationships_and_timeline}}
Audience knowledge is not necessarily character knowledge.

<Episode outline>
{{previous_hook_core_events_actions_and_ending_state}}

<Appearing characters and full profiles>
{{profiles_arcs_behavior_goals_and_relationships}}

RULES
1. Current states only:
- Include only actual appearing characters for whom the input supports a judgment.
- Name a current phase such as "actively pursuing", "hesitating before confession" or "beginning to gain confidence", not "eventually becomes a husband" or "career peak".
- Show the starting state, changes caused by this episode and the emotion/action intention left at its end.
2. Timeline before future information:
- The episode outline and recap chronology define current facts.
- Future relationships, identities, abilities and final growth in full profiles are arc references, not current knowledge or state.
- Resolve conflicts using already occurred events confirmed by the outline; unconfirmed information belongs under unknown.
3. Separate audience and character information:
- Known means actually learned, experienced or reasonably inferred by that character at that time.
- A backstage truth available to the audience through global settings remains unknown to an uninformed character.
- Full author profiles do not grant characters omniscience.
4. Serve this episode's writing:
- Emotional tone must identify concrete pressure, expectation, caution, excitement or a turn, including changes during the episode.
- Speech style must specify tone, sentence patterns, initiative/passivity, differences by interlocutor and behavior under tension/conflict.
- Knowledge boundaries must prevent premature secrets and expose usable information gaps.
5. Concise and executable:
- Keep only episode-useful information, not the full biography.
- Add no unsupported background, powers, relationships or events.
- Keep minor characters accurate without expanding their full arcs.

SELF-CHECK
Actual cast only; current rather than final arc stage; emotions tied to episode events and change; usable speech guidance rather than generic personality; audience/character knowledge separated; no premature later events; no unsupported additions.

OUTPUT
No analysis, explanation, Markdown fences or extras. One complete <DynamicProfile> block per character, ordered by first appearance or dramatic importance.

<DynamicProfile>
角色：{name}
是否为重要角色且第一次出现：是/否（如为“否”，可简要注明原因）
弧线阶段：{current phase, not the final outcome}
情绪基调：{one sentence describing the main emotional state and change}
说话风格：{executable tone, syntax, initiative and emotional delivery}
信息边界：已知——{what this character knows now}；未知——{what they do not know, even if the audience or others do}
</DynamicProfile>
```

The Chinese field labels in the output template are retained for compatibility: character; important character's first appearance (yes/no); arc stage; emotional tone; speech style; knowledge boundary (known/unknown).

## Prompt B: fair information in misunderstandings


## Prompt C: comic timing and callbacks

```text
You are a comedy pacing editor. Design setup → expectation → deflection → consequence → callback. Not every paragraph needs a joke.
<scene_or_outline>{{text}}</scene_or_outline>

Mark setup information, expectations, turn, reaction, later costs, callback elements. Check explained jokes, repeated structures, clever-dialogue-only humor, callbacks without new meaning. Suggest cuts/pauses, preserving quiet room for emotion and plot.
```

## Prompt D: boundaries and character dignity

```text
You are a comedy ethics/tone reviewer. Check who is mocked, power differences, and agency.
<text>{{text}}</text>
<tone_contract>{{tone}}</tone_contract>

Identify trauma, identity, body, poverty, disadvantage used as sole punchlines; all costs falling on one character. Distinguish characters offending each other from the work endorsing offense. Offer self-exposure, power reversal, absurd rules, cognitive gaps, character pushback. Preserve author choice while making reader risks explicit.
```

## Tests

- Mistakes follow character logic, not joke-driven intelligence loss.
- Callbacks add context, not just repeat lines.
- Comedy need not remove real consequences/emotion.

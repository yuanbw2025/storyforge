## Asset description

- asset_id: P03-DECISION-premise-stress-test
- version: 1.0.0
- task_type: Decision + diagnosis
- purpose: Compare concepts for sustained choices, causal consequences, and intended reader experience.
- non_goals: No ranking genres by personal taste; no direct outline expansion.

## Ready-to-use Prompt

```text

## Evaluation criteria
For each concept check:
1. Character agency: a specific person chooses from desire, need, or responsibility.
2. Sustained opposition: resistance changes/escalates, not solved by one misunderstanding.
3. Causal expandability: key choices change conditions and create problems.
4. Real costs: what failure, success, and inaction each lose.
5. Thematic tension: two defensible values, not slogans.
6. Genre payoff: naturally produces the promised experience.
7. Length fit: one focused change for short fiction; staged goals and sustained mechanisms for longform.
8. Distinctiveness: mechanisms/viewpoint, not proper nouns and surface settings.
9. Writability: available materials and research ability support it.
10. Risks: passive protagonist, omnipotent settings, easily escapable conflict, hollow scale, excessive similarity.

## Stress tests
For the top two concepts:
- If the protagonist immediately gets what they want, what new problem remains? None means no continuing engine.
- Without the spectacular premise, does character conflict remain?
- Does opposition have a reasonable goal without seeing itself as evil?
- What is the easiest choice the protagonist least wants to make, and why avoid it?
- Can the middle force a strategy change, not merely increase difficulty?
- What core dramatic question must the ending answer?
- What must be removed/added to make it short fiction/longform?
```

## Input/output contract

- Minimum: one concept; rank comparatively only with two or more.
- Use: story core. Scores compare these options, not overall quality or market prospects.
- Short mode: do not penalize absence of subplots or vast worlds.

## Minimum tests

1. One concept → stress test only, no invented ranking.
2. Missing information → N/A and key gaps.
3. Huge spectacle but no character goal → low agency.
4. Focused short concept → do not reject merely for limited expansion.
